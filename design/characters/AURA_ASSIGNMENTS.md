# Aura assignments (PREVIEW, not in the game yet)

Made by `tools/aura_assign.py` from your renamed stands; shown in Studio on `Workspace.AuraAssignPreview` (west of the aura showcase).
Common and Uncommon wear no aura. Every Rare hero wears your Rare aura (the 'All Rare Characters' stand) recoloured to its theme: its particle texture is a white copy of the original shape (asset 87752697532704), tinted per hero, 30 particles/s per body part, brightness 2.

Why: **named** = you named the hero on that stand; **nickname** = you did not name them, so they keep the aura they had under their in-game name; **pick** = neither, so I chose one that suits them.
