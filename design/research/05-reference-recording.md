## What the owner said after playing our build s3-03

1. The HUD at join reads cluttered and overlapping; stat panels are big and mostly empty.
2. Hint and alert boxes block the screen and are not clear.
3. Shop, food shop and the other pages are a little too big and need more polish.
4. The catch reveal card is weak and covers the hero.
5. The throw meter and the whole UI are less flashy than the reference: kids want juice.
6. The map is too small, the lane too short and narrow, the borders sparse and generic.
7. No throw animation; the food flies and lands too fast; the hero appears with no suspense.
8. Big heroes (Titan) do not sit properly on their pedestals.

## The reference HUD (sheet 1)

- Almost nothing on screen: two small icon buttons on the left edge (Index, Shop), one on the
  right (Animals), each about 5% of the screen height with a tiny caption.
- Stats are TEXT with an icon, bottom-left, no panel behind them: power, then money.
- Notices are compact cards at the top centre (icon, two short lines, one action button):
  "NEW FOOD UNLOCKED! You can now buy ORANGE [SHOP]". They do not cover the play area.
- Windows (index, shop, my animals, food shop) take roughly 35 to 45% of the screen width,
  bold title bar, a red X, dense cards. The welcome-back popup is about 20% of the width.

## The reference throw sequence (sheet 3), about 22 seconds

| t | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0 | Fade through black into "throw mode". Camera behind the character at the lane mouth. HUD gone except the power stat. |
| 1 to 4 | A VERTICAL meter beside the character, red at the bottom to green at the top, with segment lines. A marker slides up and down by itself; a "+7 / +10 / +4" bonus number rides beside it. "TAP TO THROW!" under it. |
| 5 | Tap. The bar fills, "+20 POWER", "PERFECT!". Cinematic black bars slide in top and bottom. |
| 6 | Close side shot: the character winds up and throws; the food leaves with a white streak. |
| 7 to 9 | Chase camera behind the food down the lane. Top centre: a LUCK multiplier that climbs as it flies ("1.18x", "2.36x", "2.56x LUCK") and the zone name on a ribbon ("Forest"). |
| 10 to 14 | The food has landed, small in the distance. Top centre: "1 in 1". The camera pushes in slowly. The counter starts to climb ("1 in 2"). |
| 15 to 17 | Camera low beside the food, food large in the foreground. An animal walks in beside it, sparkling. The counter keeps climbing ("1 in 3", "1 in 4", "1 in 10"). At the bottom the name spells itself out: "___________", "____A____A", "KO_KA_U__A". |
| 18 | Flash and burst. "LUCKY!" over the counter, which lands on its final value ("1 in 31") with a red burst behind it. Bottom: variant in small caps ("DIAMOND"), the name, the income ("$24/s"). |
| 19 to 21 | Hold on the animal; "tap to continue". |
| 22 | White flash back to the plot. |

What makes it work: the player does nothing for 12 seconds and still watches, because two numbers
keep moving (luck in flight, odds after landing) and the name is a puzzle that solves itself.

## Training (sheet 2)

Pull-up animation on the bar, "+1 XP" pops, a level bar at the bottom, "LEVEL UP! Throw Power +5"
in big text, a STOP button under the character.

## The reference world

One long corridor per view: brick cliff walls on both sides taller than the camera, grass on top,
trees and props along the foot of the walls all the way down, a white line across the lane at each
zone boundary, the far end lost in haze. The corridor is what makes the lane read as very long.
